# vsl

Video Sales Letter — **vídeos de vendas**. Canonical term: **VSL** (do not translate).

Talking-head with punctual cards. Default canvas follows the bruto: **16:9** or **9:16**. Same standing taste as long-form (cards on the **right**, not over the face; headlines ALL CAPS heavy Coolvetica, supporting text sentence case, each card visually distinct, overlay **60 fps**). On 9:16, keep cards on the right in the lower third so the face stays clear. Marked **tela cheia** stretches cover the whole frame until **volta ao normal**, with event sfx as needed.

When the speech is a sales argument, cards follow **what Igor actually said** (problem, proof, offer). Do not invent price, guarantee, or CTA copy.

Captions: `INFORMAÇÃO AUSENTE` (ask if this stage needs them).

Cut feel: `INFORMAÇÃO AUSENTE` (P7).

Offer/end-card layout: `INFORMAÇÃO AUSENTE` until Igor shows a print or describes it.
