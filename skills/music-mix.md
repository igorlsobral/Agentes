# music-mix

Use when adding or leveling a music **bed** (loop under the voice). One-shots (swoosh, whoosh, event sfx on **tela cheia**) live in [`sfx/`](../sfx/README.md) and [`full-screen-visual.md`](full-screen-visual.md), not here.

## Do

1. Take the file path Igor gives, or a file in `music/`.
2. Mix with FFmpeg. Default level **−23 dB** until Igor adjusts.
3. Do not duck/EQ inventively unless he asks.
4. Partial render of the mix when the picture is unchanged.
5. Preview in `projects/<clip>/previews/`.
